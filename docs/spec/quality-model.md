# Quality model

The normative definition of pass 1 of [scoring.md](scoring.md), the
`QualityScore`: what a soul's quality score and tier are, exactly, in parameter
set `yata-quality-v1`. The decision is ADR-0024. The derivation, the rejected
alternatives, and the numbers behind every threshold are in the paper,
`papers/quality-model-v1/paper.md`. The structural claims are proved in
`formal/lean/`, and every number on this page is generated by
`formal/calibration/` (ADR-0023).

Nothing on this page is _implemented_ in `yata-core` yet. It is _designed_, with
_tested_ reference computations: the calibration program and the Lean proofs.

## Domain

v1 scores **six-star souls** (C). The value tables and the roll rules are
sourced for six stars only (A); a soul below six stars has no v1 quality score,
and the application says so instead of showing a number.

## Features

For each sub-attribute `a` of a soul, its value in **roll units** (C):

```text
e_a = S(a) / hi(a)          and e_a = 0 when a ∉ dom S
```

`hi(a)` is the largest single increment at six stars
([soul-mechanics.md](soul-mechanics.md#values)). `S(a)` is the stored value,
rounded to 10⁻⁶ display units before division, which is the tolerance
soul-mechanics uses for every comparison. One increment is between 4/5 and 1
roll unit (A), so a line with `h` increments has `4h/5 ≤ e_a ≤ h`.

## The catalogue

Quality is judged against a published catalogue of **archetypes** (C, provenance
_authored_). Each values four sub-attributes with weight 1, and accepts the main
attributes listed per slot. Slots 1, 3, and 5 have a fixed main attribute, which
every archetype accepts.

| Archetype     | Useful sub-attributes                      | Slot 2 mains                   | Slot 4 mains                         | Slot 6 mains                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| `output` 输出 | `AtkPercent` `Crit` `CritDmg` `Spd`        | `Spd` `AtkPercent`             | `AtkPercent`                         | `Crit` `CritDmg` `AtkPercent` |
| `hit` 命中    | `Spd` `EffectHit` `HpPercent` `DefPercent` | `Spd` `HpPercent` `DefPercent` | `EffectHit` `HpPercent` `DefPercent` | `HpPercent` `DefPercent`      |
| `resist` 抵抗 | `Spd` `EffectRes` `HpPercent` `DefPercent` | `Spd` `HpPercent` `DefPercent` | `EffectRes` `HpPercent` `DefPercent` | `HpPercent` `DefPercent`      |

Every legal main attribute of every slot is accepted by at least one archetype
(B, `Arch.coverage`). The `output` set is the analysis's four core output
attributes (Hu 2026, § 4.1); the other two are authored. The flat attributes are
useful to none.

## Utility

```text
U_p(s) = Σ_{a ∈ A_p} e_a          A_p the archetype's four attributes
```

- `0 ≤ U_p(s) ≤ 9` for every legal soul, and 9 is reached (B,
  `Archetype.utility_le_nine`, `paperSoul_utility`): six increments at maximum
  on one useful line and one on each of the other three. That is the analysis's
  theoretical output soul (Hu 2026, § 4.4), for `output`.
- The main attribute contributes nothing to `U`; it decides only which
  archetypes may judge the soul (C).

## Normalization

```text
g_p(u) = max(0, 50·u/E_p)                     for u ≤ E_p
g_p(u) = min(100, 50 + 50·(u − E_p)/(M − E_p))   for u > E_p

E_p = μ · E[K_p]        M = 9        μ = 9/10
```

`K_p` is the number of increments a +15 soul puts on the archetype's four
attributes, under the **reference measure** (A, C): the official notice's class
weights 攻击类 36%, 防御类 36%, 功能类 28%, shared equally within a class; an
initial count of 2, 3 or 4 with 1/3 each; initial sub-attributes drawn one by
one by weight without repeats; the rolls of
[soul-mechanics.md](soul-mechanics.md#roll-distribution).

- **0** is a soul with nothing useful, **50** the expected +15 soul of the
  archetype, **100** the attainable maximum (B, `g_zero`, `g_E`, `g_M`).
- `g` is bounded in [0, 100], nondecreasing, and strictly increasing on [0, M]
  (B, `g_nonneg`, `g_le_100`, `g_mono`, `g_strict`). Scaling the utility and
  both anchors together leaves it unchanged (B, `g_scale`).
- **`E_p` is exact** (D): the calibration propagates the reference measure over
  every hit count with rational probabilities. `E_p` differs slightly between
  archetypes, because 功能类 attributes weigh 28/3% each and the others 9%:
  `hit` and `resist` hold two 功能类 attributes, `output` one. The values are in
  the table below and, as exact fractions, in
  `formal/calibration/out/constants.json`.
- **μ is the mean of one increment in roll units** (C): the midpoint of [4/5,
  1]. The law of an increment is open (A); `E_p`, and so every score, depends on
  it only through its mean (B: `U_p` is a sum of increments). The official
  site's averages give 0.907–0.913; the paper's sensitivity table gives the
  effect of such a mean on the test vectors.

## Quality

For a soul `s` in slot `k` with main `m`, over the archetypes `p` that accept
`m` on `k` (C):

```text
score_p(s) = g_p(U_p(s))
tier_p(s)  = the tier of score_p(s) and specialized_p(s), below
quality(s) = the best (tier_p, score_p): higher tier first, then higher score;
             a tie keeps catalogue order
best-fit   = the archetype that gave quality(s)
```

The score is displayed to one decimal; tiers use the exact value.

**Components shown with the total.** Only the total is a mathematical component;
the others explain it and are never added into it:

| Field       | Definition, under the best-fit archetype                                                                        |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `total`     | `score_p(s)`                                                                                                    |
| `archetype` | the best-fit archetype, and `score_p` under every accepted archetype                                            |
| `depth`     | `100 · max_{a ∈ A_p} e_a / 6`: the deepest useful line against its maximum                                      |
| `breadth`   | `100 · (D − 1)/3`, `D = U² / Σ e_a²`, 0 when `U = 0`: how many useful lines carry the utility, from one to four |
| `slot_fit`  | which archetypes accept the main attribute                                                                      |
| `growth`    | below +15: `g_p(U_p(s) + μ · E[ΔK])`, the score of the expected +15 utility                                     |

`depth` counts only useful lines, so concentration on a useless attribute earns
nothing. `breadth` depends only on the shape of the useful values, not their
size, so it does not count the total twice.

**Growth** is exact under the reference measure. For a soul with `r = 5 − ⌊ℓ/3⌋`
rolls left and the set `P` of sub-attributes present, the expected number of
further useful increments is `E[ΔK] = f(r, P)`:

```text
f(0, P) = 0
f(r, P) = r · |P ∩ A_p| / 4                                    if |P| = 4
f(r, P) = Σ_a w(a) · ([a ∈ A_p] + f(r − 1, P ∪ {a}))          if |P| < 4
```

with `w(a)` the reference weight of one draw. At +15, `growth` is `total`.

## Tiers

```text
specialized_p(s)  ⟺  ∃ a ∈ A_p.  e_a > 5

tier_p(s) = UR    if score_p(s) > t_UR,p
          = SP    else if specialized_p(s) and score_p(s) ≥ c_SSR,p
          = SSR   else if score_p(s) ≥ c_SSR,p
          = SR    else if score_p(s) ≥ c_SR,p
          = R     else if score_p(s) ≥ c_R,p
          = N     otherwise

c_R,p = g_p(E_p) = 50    c_SR,p = g_p(5μ)    c_SSR,p = g_p(7μ)    t_UR,p = g_p(M − 1)
```

The band edges are the same **utility milestones** for every archetype; their
scores differ between archetypes in the second decimal, because `E_p` does.

What each tier means, in words that match the definitions exactly:

- **N**: below the expected soul.
- **R**: at least the expected soul for its archetype.
- **SR**: at least five useful increments' worth at mean value.
- **SSR**: at least seven useful increments' worth at mean value.
- **SP**: an SSR-level soul with more than five roll units on one useful line.
  Such a line has taken all five rolls (B, `six_hits_of_gt_five`), so SP is
  decidable from values alone. A single perfect line with nothing useful beside
  it reaches `g(6)`, which is below `c_SSR`, so it is never SP (B,
  `single_line_not_SP`).
- **UR**: within one roll unit of the attainable maximum. Every UR soul has all
  nine increments on useful lines (B, `UR_structure`), and every non-UR soul
  scores strictly below every UR soul (B, `UR_score_gt`).

Precedence is UR, then SP, then the band (B, `tier_UR_iff`, `tier_SP_iff`,
`tier_cases`). At or above `c_SSR` a soul is SSR, SP or UR; no lower band
appears there (B, `high_tier`).

**The exposed order is tier first, then score** (C). UR is first in it and in
score order alike. SP is above SSR in tier order, but an SSR soul may have a
higher score than an SP soul, by at most `t_UR − c_SSR`; SP marks a kind of
excellence, not a higher number.

**Monotone under dominance** (B, `score_mono`, `score_strict`,
`tier_mono_dominance`): a soul no worse on any useful attribute of an archetype
has a score and a tier no lower under it, and a strictly better one scores
strictly higher.

**Fair across archetypes.** Each archetype's 50 is its own expected soul, so no
archetype gains because its attributes are drawn less often: a rarer set of
attributes has a lower `E_p`, and the same utility then scores the same distance
above its own expectation. Where the reference measure treats two archetypes
alike, the scales are identical (B, `score_relabel`): `hit` and `resist` differ
only by `EffectHit` and `EffectRes`, of one class, and have the same `E_p` and
the same tier rates (D, asserted). `output` differs from them in class makeup;
the residual difference is reported in the paper.

## Versioning

A score is shown with `yata-quality-v1`. Any change to the catalogue, μ, the
anchors, the thresholds, the reference measure, or the mechanics they rest on is
a new model version. Scores of different versions are not compared
([scoring.md](scoring.md#parameter-set)).
